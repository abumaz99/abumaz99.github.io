Privacy

Delete your Nomi data

Last updated: 20 October 2026

In the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Choose Delete all data (or the equivalent delete control).
  3. Confirm permanent deletion.

This permanently removes everything Nomi stored on this iPhone. There is no account and no cloud copy to delete.

If you cannot open the app

Uninstall Nomi. That removes the app's private storage. If you later restore an encrypted iPhone backup that included Nomi data, those entries can come back with the restore. Nomi does not keep a separate copy in CloudKit or iCloud Drive.
