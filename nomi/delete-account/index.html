Abu Mazhar Privacy Support

Delete your Nomi data

In the app

  1. Open Settings & privacy.
  2. Choose Delete data and account.
  3. Confirm permanent deletion. A signed-in user may be asked to sign in again.

This removes local app data and, when signed in, deletes the authentication account and its cloud metrics, check-ins, notes, and consent records. Limited operational security records without health values may remain for a short period needed for abuse prevention and audit.

If you cannot access the app

Email abumaz99@hotmail.com from the address on the account with the subject “Account deletion”. Identity will be verified before deletion. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Guest users

Guest data is stored only on the device. Use the in-app deletion control, or remove the app and any applicable device backup.
