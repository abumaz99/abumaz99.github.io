Nomi privacy notice

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who is responsible

Abu Mazhar (London, United Kingdom) publishes Nomi. Contact: abumaz99@hotmail.com.

What the app handles

Nomi for iPhone stores daily steps, sleep duration, mood, energy, stress, tags, optional private notes, and consent records on this iPhone. There is no sign-in and no account. Nomi does not collect raw health samples, heart rate, location, contacts, microphone recordings, or advertising identifiers, and does not send your check-ins or notes to an AI or model provider.

Your choices

Health access and reminders are separate, optional choices. You can choose the reminder time. The reminder does not include your check-in or health details. Declining either one does not prevent local check-ins.

Health

If you allow it, Nomi reads steps and sleep from Apple HealthKit with read-only permission and reduces them to daily totals. Those totals stay on this iPhone and are only shown back to you. Nomi does not write to Apple Health, and health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

Sharing

Nomi does not send your data to the developer or to any server, and does not use analytics. Data is not sold.

Export and restore

In Settings you can export your data as a CSV file or a JSON backup through the iPhone share sheet, and restore from a JSON backup you pick. If you export, you control where the file goes; Nomi does not upload it. Only the JSON backup can be restored.

Retention and security

Check-ins and daily health summaries are kept in full on this iPhone. Nomi does not trim or expire them. They stay until you delete them in the app or uninstall Nomi. They are kept in the app's private storage and protected until the first unlock after the phone starts. They are included in the phone's standard encrypted iOS backup, so restoring the phone can bring them back. Nomi does not copy them to CloudKit or iCloud Drive.

Your rights

Depending on UK data-protection law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing, and may complain to the ICO. Because your data stays on your iPhone, you can export it or delete it yourself in Settings. You can add, change, or remove a check-in for today or any of the previous six days. Removing a check-in leaves that day's steps and sleep in place. Older check-ins stay until you delete your data. You can also email abumaz99@hotmail.com.

Changes

If a later version sends any of your data off this iPhone, this notice will be updated before that version is released.

Safety and scope

Nomi is general wellbeing software, not a medical service or medical device. It does not diagnose or provide crisis counselling. In immediate danger call 999 or go to A&E. Samaritans are available on 116 123.
